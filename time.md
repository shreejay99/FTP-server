# 🕰 Daily Balanced Routine – README

A structured yet realistic daily routine designed to balance **DSA preparation**, **development work**, **fitness**, and **mental well-being**, while maintaining a healthy Indian lifestyle.

---

## 🌙 Sleep (Fixed & Protected)
**12:30 AM – 8:30 AM**  
- 8 hours of uninterrupted sleep  
- Non-negotiable foundation of the routine

---

## 🌅 Morning Block

### 8:30 – 9:00 AM
- Wake up & freshen up  
- Light stretching + sunlight exposure  
- ❌ No phone scrolling

### 9:00 – 9:30 AM
- Tea / coffee ☕  
- Light breakfast (poha / oats / eggs / fruits)

---

## 🧠 Deep Work Block 1 — DSA
**9:30 – 11:30 AM (2 hours)**

Focus on high-energy, deep thinking tasks:
- DSA concepts & implementation  
- Problem solving (LeetCode / GFG)  
- Pomodoro technique (50–10)

---

## 🚿 Reset + Light Tasks
**11:30 AM – 1:00 PM**
- Bath  
- Light household chores  
- Short walk / relaxation

---

## 🍛 Lunch
**1:00 – 1:30 PM**
- Proper Indian lunch  
- No phone usage  
- Eat calmly and mindfully

---

## 💻 Dev Block — Build & Explore
**1:30 – 3:30 PM (2 hours)**

Low-pressure creative work:
- Project development  
- Feature building  
- Learning new tools / frameworks

---

## 😴 Power Reset
**3:30 – 4:00 PM**
- Power nap or eyes-closed rest  
- ❌ No scrolling

---

## 🧠 Deep Work Block 2 — DSA / Revision
**4:00 – 5:30 PM (1.5 hours)**

- DSA revision  
- Timed practice  
- Weak-area improvement

---

## 🍵 Break + Chores
**5:30 – 6:30 PM**
- Snacks (fruit, chana, peanuts)  
- Family time  
- Light chores

---

## 🏋️ Gym (Fixed)
**6:30 – 8:00 PM**
- Strength training + cardio  
- Phone away  
- Focus on consistency, not intensity

---

## 🚶 Cool Down
**8:00 – 9:00 PM**
- Shower  
- Relaxation  
- Light walk

---

## 🍽 Dinner
**9:00 – 9:30 PM**
- Light dinner (protein + vegetables)  
- Avoid overeating

---

## 🌙 Light Productivity
**9:30 – 10:45 PM**
- Reading  
- Light coding  
- Planning for tomorrow  
- Skill-based videos

---

## 📓 Wind Down
**10:45 PM – 12:00 AM**
- Journaling  
- Music / calm content  
- Screens off by 12

---

## 😴 Sleep
**12:30 AM**

---

## 🔑 Final Notes
- ~**3.5 hours DSA** + **2 hours Dev** daily  
- **Sleep and Gym are non-negotiable**  
- If a day goes bad → **never sacrifice sleep or gym**  
- Aim for consistency, not perfection

---

## 🎯 Goal
Build long-term discipline, technical growth, and mental stability without burnout.
